# Sample Deck Notes

Slide 1: Project overview. Goal is to turn internal demo material into an agent-ready knowledge base.

Slide 2: Users want fast answers from the agent, but the deck mixes screenshots, roadmap notes, and UAT findings.

Slide 3: UAT screenshots show two failures: permission prompts blocked automation, and large screenshots were hard to read on mobile.

Slide 4: Model comparison shows the larger model gives better summaries, but latency and cost are higher.

Slide 5: Final recommendation: create a knowledge base with page analysis, keyword index, risk/action index, and clear answer boundaries.
